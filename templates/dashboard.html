AI Screening Process

  • Resume text is extracted from PDF or DOCX.
  • Candidate information and technical skills are identified.
  • ATS compatibility is calculated using multiple resume factors.
  • NLP analyzes similarity between the resume and job descriptions.
  • Required and missing skills are identified.
  • Jobs are ranked according to the overall match score.
